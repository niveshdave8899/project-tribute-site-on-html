mahatma gandhi

1869 - 1948

father of the nation

Mohan Das Karamchand Gandhi 2 october 1869 - 30 january 1948 was an indian lawyer, anti-colonial naionalist, andpolitical ethicist who employed nonviolent resistance to lead the succesful campaign for india's independence from british rule, and in turn inspire movement for civil rights and freedom across the world. the honorific "mahatma" (sanskrit:"great soul", "venerable") was first applied to gandhi in 1914 in south africa and is now used through out the world. in india, gandhi is also called bapu (gujrati: "father") and gandhiji, adding the hindi suffix ji as a mark of respect. gandhi was trained in the law at the inner temple, london, and was called to the bar in 1891. after two unsuccesful attempts to stablish a law practice. he moved to south africa in 1893 to work as a legal representative for an indian firm. it was in south africa that gandhi raised a family, and first employed nonviolent resistance.
